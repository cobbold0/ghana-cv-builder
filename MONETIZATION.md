# Monetization

## Model

Freemium with one-off, cedi-priced purchases. Build and preview free; pay to download a clean, professional PDF. No auto-renewing subscriptions at launch — MoMo users strongly prefer one-off payments.

## Pricing (launch hypothesis)

| Plan | Price | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Free | GHS 0 | Unlimited editing, 3 basic templates, watermarked PDF, public share link |
| Single CV | GHS 25 | Clean PDF for one CV, all templates, unlimited re-downloads of that CV, 10 AI edits |
| 30-Day Pass | GHS 50 | Unlimited CVs and cover letters, all templates, 50 AI edits/day, for 30 days |
| Pro Review | GHS 150 | 30-Day Pass + human review of CV by a recruiter within 48h |

Prices stored as pesewas; adjust after testing. Anchor against cyber-café CV typing (GHS 20–100).

## Payment methods

Via Paystack:
- MTN Mobile Money
- Telecel Cash
- AirtelTigo Money
- Visa / Mastercard

Paystack fees (~1.95% local) absorbed into price.

## Additional revenue

- **Student / NSS discount codes** distributed through campus ambassadors (20% off).
- **Institutional licences**: universities, career services and NGOs buy bulk passes (e.g. 100 passes for GHS 3,000).
- **Employer / recruitment agency partnerships**: featured "Apply with your CV" integration, referral fees.
- **Print partners**: order printed copies from partner shops; revenue share.
- **Affiliate**: professional courses and certifications (e.g. online courses relevant to skills gaps).

Avoid display ads — they slow pages and hurt trust.

## Growth levers

- Watermark reads "Made with Ghana CV Builder" + URL — free users market the product.
- Referral: give a friend GHS 5 off, earn 5 free AI edits.
- WhatsApp share of public CV link.
- Seasonal pushes: NSS postings, graduation season, GES/GHS recruitment windows.

## Unit economics (targets)

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Free → paid conversion | 5–8% of downloaders |
| Average order value | GHS 35 |
| AI cost per paid user | < GHS 2 |
| Infra cost per 1,000 MAU | < GHS 50 |
| CAC (paid channels) | < GHS 10 |

## Metrics to track

- Checkout started → payment success rate, by payment method.
- Conversion by template and entry page.
- Refund / dispute rate (< 1%).
- Revenue by plan and channel.

## Policies

- Refund within 7 days if the PDF could not be downloaded.
- Clear receipt via SMS/email with Paystack reference.
- Prices shown inclusive; no hidden fees.
